How to Cancel Bumble Subscription (Boost, Premium) — 2026

HomeTinderBumbleHingeBadooRefunds

Bumble Boost and Premium are auto-renewing subscriptions. Cancel where you paid:

iPhone (App Store billing)

  1. Settings → your name → Subscriptions
  2. Tap Bumble → Cancel Subscription

Android (Google Play billing)

  1. Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions
  2. Bumble → Cancel subscription

Web (bumble.com billing)

  1. Log in → settings/gear icon → Manage your subscription
  2. Cancel / turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before renewal

Good to know

Bumble also sells one-time purchases (Spotlight, SuperSwipe) — those are not subscriptions and don't renew. Only Boost/Premium recur. After cancelling, premium features stay active until the paid period ends; unused time is generally not refunded.
