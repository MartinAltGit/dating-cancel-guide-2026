How to Cancel Tinder Subscription (Plus, Gold, Platinum) — 2026

HomeTinderBumbleHingeBadooRefunds

Tinder subscriptions (Plus, Gold, Platinum) renew automatically. The cancellation path depends on where you originally paid.

If you subscribed on iPhone

  1. Open Settings → tap your name at the top
  2. Subscriptions → tap Tinder
  3. Cancel Subscription and confirm

If you subscribed on Android

  1. Open the Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions
  2. Subscriptions → Tinder → Cancel subscription

If you subscribed on Tinder.com

  1. Log in at tinder.com → profile icon → Manage Payment Account
  2. Turn off Auto-Renew (do this at least 24 hours before the renewal date)

What happens after cancelling

You keep premium features until the end of the current billing period. Future charges stop. Deleting the app or deleting your profile alone may not stop billing — always confirm in the store or account settings and keep a screenshot of the cancellation.
