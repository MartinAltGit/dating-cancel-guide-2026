How to Cancel Hinge Subscription (Plus, X) — 2026

HomeTinderBumbleHingeBadooRefundsFAQ

Hinge+ and Hinge X renew automatically. Cancelling the account is not the same as cancelling the subscription — do both separately if you're leaving entirely.

iPhone

  1. Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Hinge → Cancel Subscription

Android

  1. Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Hinge → Cancel subscription

Web (hinge.co)

  1. Settings → Manage subscription → cancel / disable auto-renew

Leaving Hinge completely?

Order matters: cancel the subscription first, then delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete. Deleting the account first can make it harder to find the subscription afterwards (the store subscription still exists and can still charge).
