Dating App Refunds: What Actually Works in 2026

HomeTinderBumbleHingeBadooRefunds

Short answer: refunds for unused subscription time are rare, but not impossible. Here is what realistically works.

What usually does NOT work

  • Asking the app for a refund "because I didn't use it" — most terms explicitly state no refunds for partially used periods
  • Deleting the app and expecting the charge to stop (it won't)

What sometimes works

  1. App store refund requests — Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) and Google Play both have refund request flows. Approval is case-by-case; it works most often within 48 hours of an unexpected charge.
  2. Accidental renewal right after it happened — contact the service's support immediately; some issue goodwill refunds for same-day renewal charges.
  3. Unauthorized charges — if you never subscribed or the charge came after a confirmed cancellation, dispute with your bank/card issuer with your cancellation screenshot as evidence.

Prevention beats refunds

Set a calendar reminder 2 days before every renewal, and cancel through the store settings the moment you decide you're done — not when the next charge hits.
